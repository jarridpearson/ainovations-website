Request Everward Account and Data Deletion

Everward users may request deletion of their Everward account and associated app data by emailing AInovations.

Request Account and Data Deletion

When a deletion request is processed, AInovations will delete or anonymize Everward account records and associated app data, including profile information, priorities, decisions, AI review history, AI analysis records, credit ledger records, and subscription-related app records where deletion is technically and legally permitted.

Some limited records may be retained when required for legal, security, fraud prevention, tax, accounting, dispute resolution, or compliance purposes. Any retained records will be kept only as long as necessary for those purposes.

Account and data deletion requests are typically reviewed within 30 days.
