THE QUEST

Nine levels stand between you and the beast king. Each one holds a piece of the Triforce, and a guardian that will not give it up. Find all eight, and the ninth level opens on Death Mountain.

You start with nothing. There is a cave on the first screen. Go in.

CONTROLS

MOVE

←↑↓→ or WASD

SWORD

X — at full health it throws a beam

ITEM

Z — uses whatever is in the B slot

INVENTORY

Enter — pick your B item

SOUND

M to mute

TOUCH

On-screen pad and buttons appear on phones and tablets

THINGS WORTH KNOWING

  • Walls that look solid are not always solid. Carry bombs.
  • Some trees burn. A lantern is worth the coin.
  • Certain gravestones will shift if your arms are strong enough.
  • Still water answers a pipe.
  • An old man will trade you a better blade once you can survive one.
  • Dark rooms are not empty rooms.
