Employee experience

The platform extends beyond administration.

Employees use Everward to define priorities, track decisions, measure activity, review progress, and receive AI-assisted perspective while retaining responsibility for their own decisions.

  • Priorities Define important outcomes, responsibilities, goals, and areas of focus.
  • Decision tracking Record important choices, context, expected outcomes, and follow-up information.
  • AI-assisted analysis Review potential benefits, risks, considerations, and tradeoffs without having AI make the decision.
  • Trackables and progress indicators Measure relevant activity and see whether progress is rising, steady, or declining.
  • Decision calibration Employees predict outcomes before they happen and check in afterward. Everward tracks whether each person tends to be overconfident, underconfident, or well-calibrated, and rolls that up into org-wide reporting.
  • Weekly and monthly reviews Review recent activity, recognize patterns, and identify areas that may need attention.
