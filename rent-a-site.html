Questions

Rent-a-Site FAQ

What does the $100/month include?
The initial website build, hosting, security and uptime monitoring, Google Analytics with a monthly report, search-engine basics done right, and two hours of ongoing work every month — a new article, content updates, or improvements we recommend. No setup fee, no surprise charges.
What is the two-hour monthly scope?
Each month includes up to two hours of active work on your site beyond hosting and monitoring — typically one published article or one batch of content updates, plus our recommendations. Bigger requests are quoted separately or queued for the following month, so the price never surprises you.
Will my site rank #1 on Google?
Nobody can honestly promise rankings, and we won't. What we deliver: a fast, properly built site that search engines can index, set up the right way — titles, descriptions, local business info, analytics — plus fresh content monthly. For businesses starting with no web presence, that alone is a real, visible difference.
Is there a contract?
Month to month. Cancel anytime. That's part of the point — $100 flat, nothing to worry about.
Who owns the site? Can I buy it?
AInovations builds and operates the site and everything behind it — that's what keeps it $100 a month. Your subscription covers the build, hosting, updates, and support. Want to own your site outright? Just like Rent-an-App, that's a conversation, not a menu item — reach out and we'll talk.
What happens if I cancel?
The site and hosting are part of the service, so the site retires when the subscription ends — and we'll export your content and hand over your domain name so nothing you paid for is lost.
Why is it only $100?
This is our Hero Program pricing for the first 20 clients — businesses willing to grow with us while we grow. Hero clients keep the $100 rate for as long as they stay subscribed. After 20, the price goes up for new clients.
