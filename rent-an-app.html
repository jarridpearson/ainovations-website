Questions

Rent-an-App FAQ

What does Rent-an-App include?
Everything it takes to run your app: the build, hosting, updates, maintenance, and support — all covered by one flat monthly rate. You never pay a five-figure development bill.
Is it a real app or a website shortcut?
A real native iPhone and Android app, published on the App Store and Google Play under your own developer accounts — your business name on the listing.
Who owns the app?
AInovations builds and operates the app and its platform — that's what keeps the monthly rate affordable. Your agreement covers the license, hosting, updates, and support for your business. If you're interested in a different arrangement, reach out and we'll talk.
How does the AI chat agent work?
On Mid and Pro plans, we run an AI chat agent trained on your business — hours, services, pricing, policies — so it can answer customer questions and capture leads around the clock. We manage the AI with nothing for you to set up. Prefer to run it on your own AI provider account? Bring your own API key and we'll wire it in.
How do in-app payments work on Pro?
You connect your own Stripe, Square, or PayPal account and your customers pay you directly. AInovations takes no percentage of your sales — you pay only your payment provider's standard processing rates plus your flat monthly rent.
What are the contract terms?
Plans are 1-year or 3-year agreements. The 3-year plan gets a lower monthly rate and the onboarding fee waived. After the initial term, agreements renew annually.
