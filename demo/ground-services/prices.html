What’s already in the price

  • Pumping the tank out completely — not “skimming the top,” which is how a cheap quote gets cheap
  • Lids back down, ground raked, gate shut
  • A look at the baffles and the filter while we’re in there, and we tell you what we saw
  • Your pump date on file, so next time you can just ask us
  • Disposal fees at the treatment plant

Why we publish this

Because you were going to call and ask anyway, and because a price you can see is a price you can trust. Most of our day used to be answering “roughly how much?” from the cab of a truck. Now it’s on the website and we can go do the work.

Commercial tanks, grease traps, holding tanks and anything over 2,000 gallons are quoted — those genuinely vary. Call and we’ll give you a number on the phone.
