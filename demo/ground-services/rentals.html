The overage rate is right there on purpose. A surprise weight bill two weeks after the truck left is the single most common complaint in this business. You’ll know the included tonnage and the per-ton rate before you book, and we weigh at a certified scale and send you the ticket.

What we can’t take

Paint, tires, batteries, appliances with refrigerant, propane tanks, asbestos. Ask us — some of it we can take separately, and we’d rather tell you now than leave the can sitting in your driveway.
