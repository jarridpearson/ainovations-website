Everward Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AInovations LLC collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use Everward.

1. Information We Collect

Everward may collect information you provide directly, including account information, user-created priorities, decisions, notes, AI review inputs, AI analysis results, and support requests.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to operate Everward, provide priority and decision-support features, generate AI-assisted summaries or diagnostics, manage subscriptions or usage limits, improve reliability, and respond to support requests.

3. AI Processing

Everward may send relevant user-provided content to AI service providers to generate summaries, reviews, diagnostics, or decision-support outputs. AI outputs are informational and should not be treated as professional, legal, financial, medical, or emergency advice.

4. Storage and Service Providers

Everward uses third-party service providers for app infrastructure, authentication, data storage, AI processing, payments, analytics, and advertising where applicable. These providers process information only as needed to support the app.

5. Advertising

Everward may use rewarded ads for free users. Advertising partners may process device or ad interaction information to load, measure, and prevent abuse of ads.

6. Payments and Subscriptions

Payments and subscriptions are handled through the applicable app store or payment provider. AInovations LLC does not directly collect full payment card details through Everward.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as needed to provide Everward, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security. Users may request account or data deletion by contacting support.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information. No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children

Everward is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Everward changes. The latest version will be posted on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, support, or data deletion requests, contact AInovations LLC at jp@ainovations.net.
