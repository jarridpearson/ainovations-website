=== OVERALL ===
Yes: every one of the ten steps can be built and run by a solo developer on Toast standard API access with no partner program, no custom integration, and no writes to Toast, but four of them do not work as written and need structural changes: reward redemption cannot be confirmed from the feed at the counter (deduct points at activation and use one shared single-use-per-phone code per reward tier), the iOS/Android geofence is a minority opt-in bonus running on an API Apple has deprecated (a QR scan or manual tap is the real check-in), Apple's per-restaurant publishing model only works for restaurants enrolled as an Organization (LLC/corp with a D-U-N-S, a domain email and a live website; a sole proprietor's Individual account cannot add AInovations to a team), and Expo's Acceptable Use Policy forbids using EAS to build apps for third parties without written permission, which must be in hand before client zero's first build. The single biggest technical risk is the attribution loop through Toast's read-only feed: guest identity exists only on takeout/delivery orders and only as whatever phone or email the guest typed at Toast's checkout, so every loyalty promise on the page reduces to "did the member use the same phone number in Toast online ordering as in the app", and nothing rung at the counter can be tied to a member by the feed at all. Second is store review: nobody has published a restaurant app that orders through SFSafariViewController, and Apple has rejected white-label apps submitted from clients' own accounts under 4.3(a). Client zero must prove, in this order, that the credential screen has no click-through terms and exposes all seven scopes, that the self-serve Manage webhooks screen exists and an order_updated arrives within a minute, that a real Toast OO order comes back with Check.customer matching the member's phone on both the express and guest checkout paths, that single-use codes are enforced online and appliedPromoCode is visible on GET, and that a build with in-app-browser ordering passes App Review under the restaurant's own Organization account. Confidence is high that it can be built, moderate that the first restaurant is live in both stores inside six weeks, and low that the geofence nudge will ever reach more than about a quarter of members.

=== PRECEDENT ===
No one has published this exact stack. The individual pieces have real precedent: several products ingest Toast orders from customer-created standard API credentials with the same webhook-plus-nightly-ordersBulk pattern (nimble-pnl, Nango, restly, pantry-iq, a Houston bar's ETL, Coursed, LineNow), and Toast's own Branded Mobile App and ChowNow publish one native app per restaurant from restaurant-owned Organization developer accounts with the vendor invited as Admin, which is the compliant 4.2.6 model the design already uses. Twilio's ISV subaccount-per-customer architecture, Stripe direct charges from React Native, and Google Wallet's aggregator/parent-issuer model are all vendor-documented for exactly this shape. What has no precedent anywhere: a loyalty app awarding points from standard-access order reads (the one restaurant marketing bot that tried it concluded guest data only exists inside individual takeout/delivery orders), a native app that opens Toast Online Ordering in SFSafariViewController/Custom Tabs (every code hit is a restaurant website linking out), and Wallet passes signed with each client's own Pass Type ID certificate (all four small-shop loyalty repos found sign every client under one shared cert, the pattern PassKit itself calls a workaround). The closest commercial analogues for the loyalty loop (Incentivio, Toast Loyalty, PassKit's Toast page) all run through the partner API, and Incentivio awards points next-day rather than instantly. The Aug 2026 community post from a bar on standard access is the single most important field report: it proves a POS promo redemption shows the code but never the guest, and Toast answered with a private ticket rather than a fix. Client zero is therefore the precedent for the whole assembly, not a repeat of someone else's.

=== DESIGN CHANGES ===
1. Rewards (item 3): deduct points at activation, not on API confirmation. Member taps Redeem, backend deducts, app shows the code with a 15-minute expiry, staff applies it. One shared single-use-per-phone Toast promo code per reward tier and one for the 'download the app, 10% off today' counter deal, each created once in Toast Web with description blank and Display on Online Menus off, Permissions Level = Any User, Start/End dates rotated monthly. Use the read API only as a reconciliation signal on Toast OO orders (Check.customer present and appliedPromoCode equal to a configured code) and read GET /config/v2/discounts (config:read) at sync time to verify each code exists and its usageType. Drop per-member code pools (no write API; codes are hand-entered one at a time), drop any 'first N guests' cap, one code per order, no kiosk redemption, free-item rewards built as BOGO/fixed item-level with Eligible # of Get Items = 1.
2. Loyalty feed (item 1): webhook + GET, never match from the webhook body. Ack order_updated with 2xx inside 2 s, enqueue the GUID, GET /orders/v2/orders/{guid} with guest.pi:read, match on E.164-normalized phone then lowercased email, accrue only for source in {Online, Branded Online Ordering, eCommerce Online ordering}, idempotent ledger keyed by order GUID + modifiedDate, reverse on voided/refundStatus. Fallback ordersBulk poll every 60-120 s over a trailing 15-minute modified window (max 5 req/s/location, always send Toast-Restaurant-External-ID), nightly per-businessDate reconciliation, alert when no webhook arrives for 15 minutes during posted hours. === PAGE CLAIMS AFFECTED ===
- Claim: points land 'a minute later'. Weakened by: the feed only carries guest identity on takeout/delivery orders placed through Toast online ordering, matching depends on the guest using the same phone number, webhooks have no SLA and the fallback poll runs every 1-2 minutes, and dine-in orders are never attributed. Safer wording: 'Order pickup or delivery through your Toast online ordering and the points show up in the app, usually within a couple of minutes.'
- Claim: 'Walk in, tap once' check-in. Weakened by: the arrival nudge needs Always location (roughly 10-25% of members grant it), runs on an API Apple has deprecated, can go silent after two weeks unopened on iOS and after a reboot or app-sleep on Android, and is a 100-200 m 'nearby' signal rather than proof of presence. Safer wording: 'Walk in and check in with one tap or a quick scan of the code on the table.'
- Claim: 'Get near your place and it can pop up on the lock screen'. Weakened by: on iPhone the Wallet card surfaces silently (no alert, no sound) within about 100 m; on Android the nearby notice only fires if the guest has turned on Wallet notifications and always-on precise location for Google Wallet, with Google choosing the distance and wording. Safer wording: 'Their wallet card can show up on the lock screen when they're close by, on phones set to allow it.'
- Claim: 'Order in your app' via Toast OO inside the app. Weakened by: the order is placed and paid on Toast's own ordering page opened in an in-app browser, the app never sees the cart or payment, and no other restaurant app is known to have passed App Review with this pattern yet. Safer wording: 'Order from your app: it opens your own Toast online ordering right inside the app, so nothing changes on your side.'
- Claim: promo-code rewards 'one use per person — Toast keeps track'. Weakened by: Toast tracks single-use codes per phone number (staff types the phone at the register; online enforcement on guest checkout is unproven), a shared code can be used once by any phone, and the app confirms counter redemptions by deducting at activation rather than by seeing who used the code. Safer wording: 'one use per phone number — Toast enforces it at the register and at online checkout.'
- Claim: 'four to six weeks' to the stores. Weakened by: it holds only for an LLC/corp that can enroll as an Organization on Apple and Google; a sole proprietor faces Apple's Individual-account limits and Google's 12-tester, 14-day closed test, and Apple organization enrollment backlogs ran 2-7 weeks in early 2026. Safer wording: 'typically four to six weeks for an LLC or corporation, mostly waiting on Apple and Google account approvals; we start the paperwork on day one.'
- Claim: memberships, tickets and merch sold in your app through your own Stripe. Weakened by: Stripe Billing adds 0.7% on recurring memberships, Apple requires renewal/cancel disclosures before the payment sheet, and Apple Pay/Google Pay arrive in a follow-up release after per-restaurant approvals. Safer wording: 'Memberships, tickets and merch are sold in your app and paid straight into your own Stripe account at Stripe's published rates.'
- Claim: texts 'signed with your name'. Weakened by: US texts go out from a local 10-digit number registered to the restaurant's business, not an alphanumeric sender name; the restaurant's name appears in the message body, and the campaign must be registered before any text can leave. Safer wording: 'Texts go out from a local number registered to your business, with your name in every message.'
- Claim: 'the carriers want your EIN once'. Weakened by: registration also needs the exact IRS legal name and address, a live website showing the business, an owner contact email on the business's own domain, a published SMS terms and privacy page, hosted screenshots of the opt-in, and 1-3 weeks of carrier vetting before the first text; it costs about $19.50 once plus $10/month.
