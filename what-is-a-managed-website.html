What Is a Managed Website?

A managed website is exactly what it sounds like: instead of buying a website (then owning every problem it ever has), you pay one flat monthly rate and someone else builds it, hosts it, watches it, and keeps it fresh. You approve what goes live; they do everything else.

What "managed" should include

  • The build itself — a real site for your business, not a template you assemble alone
  • Hosting, security, and uptime monitoring — the invisible work that keeps a site alive
  • Google Analytics and a plain-English report — who visited, what they looked at
  • Search-engine basics done right — titles, descriptions, local business info, sitemap
  • Fresh content on a schedule — because a site nobody touches slides down the rankings

What to watch for (from someone who sells this)

Three honest questions to ask any managed-website provider, including us: Is there a setup fee? (Ours: no.) Am I locked into a contract? (Ours: month-to-month, cancel anytime.) What exactly happens each month? (Ours: up to two hours of active work — an article or a batch of updates — plus all the hosting and monitoring, stated plainly so $100 is always $100.)

Why it fits small businesses specifically

A five-person shop doesn't need a webmaster; it needs a website that works while everyone's doing their real jobs. Managed is how you get that without the five-figure build-and-maintain cost — and if your business grows into wanting more, the same model scales up to your own mobile app.
