Common questions

How does Ringlatch help a HVAC company?

It answers the calls you cannot get to, gets the caller's name, number and what they need, and texts it to you within seconds. Calls like a furnace that quit at 10pm in February get flagged urgent and ring your cell.

Will it try to answer questions it should not?

No. It works from a profile you approve, never quotes a firm price, never promises an arrival time, and says plainly that it is an automated assistant if asked.

What happens if someone hangs up on it?

They get a text within seconds inviting them to reply with what they need, so you still get the lead.
