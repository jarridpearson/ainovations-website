What's not covered

These cards are for sites that show, tell, and get the call.

If the site needs any of these, it doesn't qualify for a gift card.

  • No e-sales. No online store, shopping cart, or taking payments on the site.
  • No subscriptions. No memberships, recurring billing, or paid content.
  • No back end. No customer logins, accounts, databases, or booking systems built into the site.
  • No more than the card's pages. 5 pages on the $100 card, 10 pages on the $150 card.
