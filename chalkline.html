On purpose

What we deliberately left out.

Most field-service software is judged by how much it does. Chalkline is also judged by what it refuses to do.

✕

No GPS tracking of your crew

Your people are carpenters, not delivery pins on a map. Time tracking is clock in / clock out — nothing follows anyone around.

✕

No platform cut on payments

Software that skims a percentage of your invoices is taking your margin. We charge for the tool, not a toll on your revenue.

✕

No texting your customers

Client communication goes by email — professional, documented, and never mistaken for spam.

✕

No replacing your accountant

QuickBooks stays. Chalkline runs the field work and exports clean records — your books and your accountant keep working the way they do now.

✕

No data hostage-taking

Your client list and billing history are your business. Export everything, any time, in files you can open anywhere.

✕

No AI gimmicks

Nothing in Chalkline guesses, hallucinates, or "assists." It records what happened on the job, accurately, every time.
