📸 AI Photo Diagnosis

Snap your tank. Get a diagnosis.

Something looks off but you can't quite name it. Instead of guessing — or losing livestock while you research — photograph your tank and let TankMate tell you what it's seeing.

What it spots

TankMate reads your photo for the problems that actually cost hobbyists fish and coral: algae outbreaks and which type, visible disease signs on fish, symptoms of water-quality trouble, and stress in corals or plants. It names what it sees in plain English instead of jargon.

It reads your water, too

A photo alone is a guess. TankMate factors in the water parameters you've logged, so a yellowing coral is read alongside your alkalinity trend and a stressed fish alongside your ammonia — a sharper diagnosis than eyes on a picture could give.

Then it tells you what to do

A label isn't help. Every diagnosis comes with concrete next steps you can actually act on — what to change, what to test, and what to watch — matched to your specific tank type.

Every tank, tailored

Reef, freshwater, planted, cichlid, shrimp, or axolotl — the diagnosis is tuned to what's normal and what's a warning sign for your setup, not a generic fish-tank average.
