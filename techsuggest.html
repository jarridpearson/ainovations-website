Why it feels like a gamble

Almost everywhere you can buy it, somebody else’s interest sits between you and the right answer.

Not because the gear is bad. Because of who is doing the advising, and what they get out of it.

BIG-BOX STORES

Undertrained staff, pointed at the upsell

You came in for a laptop that opens email and leave with a machine built for video editing you are never going to do.

COMMISSION FLOORS

The advice bends toward what pays

When the person advising you is paid on what you buy, the recommendation follows the commission. That is not dishonesty — it is just how the incentive works.

SHOPPING ALONE ONLINE

Endless specs and reviews you can’t trust

Contradictory roundups, padded spec sheets and reviews you can’t tell are real. You end up guessing, and the guess is usually expensive.

AND THE HONEST PART

Most people don’t know what they actually need

There is no reason they should. So they overpay for things they will never use, or underbuy and replace the whole thing in a year.
