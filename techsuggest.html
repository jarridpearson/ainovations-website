Most of the time, it is a gamble.

Not because the gear is bad. Because almost everywhere you can buy it, somebody else’s interest sits between you and the right answer.

Big-box floor staff

Often undertrained, and pointed at the upsell. You came in for a laptop that opens email and leave with a machine built for video editing you will never do.

Commission-based stores

When the person advising you is paid on what you buy, the advice bends toward whatever pays them most. That is not dishonesty — it is just how the incentive works.

Shopping online alone

Endless spec sheets, contradictory roundups and reviews you can’t tell are real. You end up guessing, and the guess is usually expensive.

And the honest part

Most people don’t know what they actually need — there is no reason they should. So they overpay for things they will never use, or underbuy and replace it in a year.
