🔎 AI Species ID

Photograph it. Know what it is.

Bought a mystery fish? Found a hitchhiker on your live rock? Not sure if that's a good plant or a weed? Point your camera and TankMate names it.

Fish, coral, and plants

TankMate identifies the livestock across the hobby — community fish, marine species, corals, and aquarium plants — from a single photo, so you're never guessing at a label on a store tank or a hitchhiker in your own.

Straight into your inventory

An identified species goes right into your tank's inventory, so your livestock list builds itself as you go — no manual typing, no misspelled names.

Know before it's a problem

Once TankMate knows what's in your tank, it can warn you. Add a species and the compatibility engine checks it against everything already living there — so an aggressive or predatory addition doesn't become an expensive surprise.
