Why this exists

You pay for all of it per person, every month, and then still track your actual money in a spreadsheet because the CRM has no idea what you paid your supplier. We built ours the other way around: customers and money, in one place, and nothing else.

What it tracks

Six things. That's the whole system.

Deliberately small. Everything here is something you already have to keep track of anyway — it just stops living in six different places.

Customers THE RECORD

  • Business name, contact, email, phone, town, website
  • Status — lead, quoted, won, active, paused, done
  • What they bought, what they pay, what term they're on
  • Notes you can pin to the top of the record
  • An activity log that writes itself when something changes
  • Search and filter, so you can find anyone in two seconds

Money in READ-ONLY

  • Every payment received, tied to the customer who sent it
  • Amount, date, invoice number, paid or still open
  • Collected all-time and currently outstanding, per customer
  • Pulled straight from the payment processor you already use

It mirrors what your processor already did. Nothing in the CRM can charge anyone or change anything in your payment account.

Money out WITH RECEIPTS

  • Date, vendor, description, category, amount, how you paid
  • Tag it to a customer when it was for a specific job
  • Billable and reimbursed flags, so nothing gets eaten
  • Photograph the receipt onto the line — stored privately

Snap it in the parking lot and it's done. Nothing to file later.

Processing fees DEDUCTIBLE

  • Your card processor's cut, in its own bucket
  • Pulled automatically, never typed by hand
  • Attributed to the customer whose payment caused it

This is the line most small businesses never capture. It's real money and it's deductible.

Mileage IRS RATE

  • Date, purpose, where from, where to, miles
  • Round-trip toggle so you log it once
  • Current IRS rate, updated each year
  • Tag the trip to a customer if it was for a job

Year-end totals THE POINT

  • Everything filtered by tax year
  • Deductible expenses, mileage value, fees paid, revenue collected
  • Profit margin per customer, and across the whole business
  • Any screen exports to a spreadsheet

The figures your accountant asks for, already added up and already attached to receipts.

The part nobody else does

Your accountant gets a login. It's free, and it doesn't use a seat.

Every other CRM makes you buy a seat for your bookkeeper, so most small businesses don't — and then spend January emailing PDFs back and forth. Yours gets their own read-only login instead.

What they can see

  • Revenue collected, by customer and by year
  • Every expense with its category and its receipt
  • Processing fees as their own deductible line
  • Mileage logs at the rate for that year
  • Profit margin per customer and overall
  • Export of all of it to a spreadsheet

What they can't

  • Change a single record — the login is read-only
  • See your customer notes or your activity log
  • Reach your payment account, because nothing here can

Which is why handing it out costs you nothing to worry about.

Questions

Straight answers

How much does it cost?
Three tiers, all on a three-year agreement with no setup fee: Essentials $100/month (up to 10 users), Tailored $200/month (15 users), Built-for-you $300/month (25 users). On a one-year it is $125, $250 and $350 a month plus a $500 setup. Every tier includes the free read-only accountant login, and it never uses one of your seats.
Does my accountant have to buy a seat?
No. Your accountant or bookkeeper gets a read-only login at no cost, and it doesn't count against your 10 users. They see the money — revenue, expenses, receipts, fees, mileage and the tax-year totals — and not your customer notes.
Does it take payments or send invoices?
No. It reads. It connects to the payment processor you already use and mirrors what already happened there — who paid, how much, when, and what the processing fee was. Nothing in the CRM can charge a customer or change anything in your payment account, which is exactly why it's safe to hand your bookkeeper a login.
How many users do I get?
Up to 10, plus the free accountant login on top of that. Need more than 10? We'll quote it.
Can I add the AI assistant?
Yes. The AI Chat Agent add-on is $50 a month and covers up to 250 chats — the same terms as everywhere else we offer it.
Is my data mine?
Yes. Every screen exports to a spreadsheet, and if you ever leave we hand you your data in full.
Does this replace my accountant?
No, and it isn't tax advice. It puts the numbers your accountant asks for in one place, already added up and already tied to receipts, so January is a download instead of a shoebox.
Is it built for my business specifically?
Yes. We set the categories, the fields and the screens up around how your business actually runs before you ever log in, and we keep adjusting them as it changes. That's the difference between this and configuring a generic CRM yourself on a Sunday.
What if I already use something?
Tell us what it is and what you keep in it. If moving your records across makes sense we'll say so, and if staying where you are makes more sense we'll say that too.
