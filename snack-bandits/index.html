Carry the loot home without dying and place it on a podium. Your living snacks sit there and print cash while you play.

Pets

75 unique pets to collect — and the rarer the pull, the bigger the boost.

75 Pets

Three egg types in every world — income, damage and agility boosts. Each egg hatches 5 different pets, and none repeat across the 75.

Pet Combiner

Feed 5 identical pets into the Pet Combiner for a guaranteed Big (3× boost). Combine five Bigs for a Huge (10×). Fewer than five risks losing them.

They follow you

Up to 3 pets follow and boost you at once — or 6 with the VIP pass. Rarer pets mean more cash, more damage, more speed.

FAQ

Good to know.

Is Snack Bandits free?

Yes — free to play on Roblox, on phone, tablet and PC. Optional passes exist but everything can be earned by playing.

How do I get a Big pet?

Buy eggs at the Pet Shop, then feed 5 identical pets into the Pet Combiner on the back wall. Five at once is a guaranteed Big.

Can I go back to earlier worlds?

Yes. The portal lets you travel to any world you've unlocked, any time, free.

How do I stop players robbing me?

Flip the shield switch on your hideout's back wall. It's free, but it's short and slow to recharge — time it well.

What are Gems for?

Beat THE CRUMB CRUSHER to earn Gems, then spend them at the portal to unlock the next world.
