How to Play Snack Bandits

Everything a new bandit needs — from your first egg to THE CRUMB CRUSHER.

Snack Bandits is easy to pick up: grow living snacks that print cash, steal rarer ones from rivals, and defend your stash. Here's how it all fits together.

The core loop

  1. Buy eggs at the Pet Shop.
  2. Your pets follow you and boost you.
  3. Steal snacks from rival hideouts.
  4. Carry loot home, then place it on a podium.
  5. Unlock more podium slots.
  6. Feed 5 pets at the Pet Combiner for a Big.
  7. Rebirth — the portal needs them.
  8. Beat the boss, then take the Portal.

Grow snacks that print cash

Your hideout has podiums, and every hideout can hold up to 8 podium slots. Place a living snack on a podium and it earns cash while you play. Unlock more slots and stack rarer snacks to grow your income. Servers hold 8 players and 8 hideouts, so there's always someone to raid — and someone eyeing yours.

Hatch and combine 75 pets

There are 75 unique pets — 15 per world, and none repeat. Each world has 3 egg types (income, damage and agility boosts), and every egg hatches from a pool of 5 different pets. Rarer pulls are stronger.

The Pet Combiner is where you power up. Feed identical pets in — each pet gives a 20% chance of success, so feeding all 5 at once is a guaranteed Big (a 3× boost). Combine five Bigs into a Huge (10×). Feed fewer than five and a failure eats the pets, so save up. Up to 3 pets follow you at once — or 6 with the VIP pass.

Steal — and survive the corridor

The good loot is out in the danger corridor: 4 danger zones full of lasers, crushers, guard bots and jump gaps. The deeper you go, the richer the reward — and the easier it is to die. Grab the loot, then get it home in one piece and onto a podium before a rival snatches it back.

Defend your stash

Every hideout has a free shield switch on the back wall. Flip it when bandits come for you. It's free, but it's timed and slow to recharge, so don't waste it — read the room and shield when it counts.

Climb the five worlds

Progress runs through five tiers: Normal → Advanced → Master → Shiny → Shimmery. Each world brings new weapons, new pets, new snacks, tougher jumps and a meaner economy. There are 25 weapons in all — 5 per world, from the Candy Cane Blade in Normal up to the Eternity Blade in Shimmery. Once you unlock a world it stays unlocked, and the portal lets you travel back any time, free.

Rebirth and beat the boss

Rebirth resets your run for the strength the portal demands. Then face THE CRUMB CRUSHER — a three-phase boss who guards the Gems you need. Beat him, earn Gems, and spend them at the portal to unlock the next world. Bring your best weapon and a damage pet.
