Our Story

A family idea that turned into a whole game. 🍪

Snack Bandits started the way the best games do — with kids describing exactly what they wanted to play.

My sons wanted a game where you could steal snacks, hunt down hidden ones, fight robots, dodge lasers, and climb across moving blocks to reach the good stuff. So instead of just nodding along, we sat down and built it together. They dreamed up the game; I wrote the code. Every idea in it came from them.

You'll see all of it in there: hideouts full of snacks to raid, a danger corridor stacked with lasers and guard bots, moving blocks and jump gaps to time just right, and living snacks you grow to print cash. Then you defend your stash, hatch pets, and portal on to tougher worlds — right up to THE CRUMB CRUSHER.

It's a goofy, chaotic heist game, and it's free on Roblox. We made it to have fun as a family — and to share it with anyone who wants to grab a mask and join in. If you play, we'd love to hear what you think.
