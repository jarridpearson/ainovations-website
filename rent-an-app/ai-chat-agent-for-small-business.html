What Can an AI Chat Agent Do for a Small Business?

Most customer questions aren’t hard — they’re just constant. What are your hours? Do you service my area? How much is a typical job? Can I get in this week? Every one of them matters to the person asking, and every one you miss is a lead that quietly books elsewhere.

What the agent actually does

An AI chat agent inside your app is trained on your business — your services, hours, pricing, service area, and policies. From there it can:

  • Answer instantly, around the clock. Evenings, weekends, holidays — the questions get answered when they’re asked, which is when buying decisions happen.
  • Capture the lead. When a conversation turns into “can you come out Tuesday?”, the agent collects the details and hands them to you instead of letting the moment pass.
  • Stay inside the fence. The agent answers from your business information — it’s not a general chatbot freelancing about your prices.

Managed for you — nothing to set up

On Rent-an-App’s Mid and Pro plans, the AI is managed for you: no accounts to create, no API keys, no usage bills to monitor. It’s included in the flat monthly rate, with a clear cap (up to 1,000 chats/month on Mid, up to 2,500 on Pro) so costs stay predictable.

Prefer to run the agent on your own AI provider account? Bring your own API key and we’ll wire it in — your usage bills at cost to your provider instead.

Where it fits
