Selling Through Your Own App: In-App Sales & Subscriptions

Marketplaces and booking platforms are happy to sell for you — in exchange for a slice of every transaction, forever. Selling through your own app keeps the relationship, the data, and the revenue where they belong: with you.

What you can sell in-app

  • Products. A catalog of what you sell (up to 100 products on the Pro plan), with checkout built in.
  • Services. Bookable, payable work — deposits, flat-rate jobs, packages.
  • Subscriptions. Recurring plans your customers subscribe to inside the app: maintenance plans, memberships, retainers, deliveries.

Your payment account, not ours

On Rent-an-App’s Pro plan, you connect your own Stripe, Square, or PayPal account and your customers pay you directly. AInovations takes no percentage of your sales — you pay your payment provider’s standard processing rates and your flat monthly rent, nothing more.

That structure matters more than it looks. Platforms that route your money through their own accounts control your payouts and take their cut off the top. When the payment account is yours, the money is yours — the app is just the storefront.

Why subscriptions are the sleeper feature

One-off sales are good; recurring revenue is better. A lawn service selling seasonal plans, a salon selling memberships, a shop selling a monthly box — subscriptions inside your own app turn regulars into predictable income without a marketplace skimming it.
