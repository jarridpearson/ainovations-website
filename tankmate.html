One app, every tank

An AI second set of eyes on your aquarium.

TankMate tailors what it tracks, the species it knows, and the compatibility rules it enforces to your exact tank type.

🐟

In-store compatibility scanner

At the store, snap a fish before you buy. TankMate checks it against your tank — size, water type, temperament, and your existing livestock — and tells you if it fits or will cause problems.

📸

AI photo diagnosis

Photograph your tank and get a plain-English read on likely problems — algae, disease signs, water-quality symptoms, coral or plant stress — with concrete next steps.

🔎

AI species ID

Don't know what you're looking at? Photograph a fish, coral, or plant and TankMate identifies it, then adds it to your inventory.

💚

Ecosystem health

Track every inhabitant, get an overall Ecosystem Health score, and see warnings about species that shouldn't cohabitate.

🧪

Water logging + Tank Health Score

Log parameters — auto-tailored per tank type — and get a single 0–100 health score with trends over time.

⚠️

Smart compatibility engine

Hobby-accurate warnings before mistakes happen — a lionfish eats small tankmates, male bettas can't share a tank, Caridina and Neocaridina shrimp want different water.

🔥

Badges & streaks

Gentle gamification that rewards consistent care — because consistency is what keeps tanks alive.

🐠

Multi-tank & freemium

Manage every tank from one account. Free for casual keepers; TankMate Pro unlocks more AI diagnoses and unlimited tanks.
