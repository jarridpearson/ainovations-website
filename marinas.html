Questions

The things you're actually thinking

I'm full. I've got a waitlist. Why would I want this?
Because slips aren't where your money is. Winterizing, shrink wrap, storage and service are — and those you lose one at a time, quietly, to whoever answered the phone first. Being full doesn't protect any of that. One haul-out job you didn't get costs more than a year of this.
My season's five months long. Why pay all year?
Fair question, and here's the honest answer: the site is doing its hardest work in the months you're closed. September through November people are looking for winterizing. February and March they're shopping slips and storage for spring. If it only ran during the season you'd miss both of the times people are actually deciding.
Who's going to keep it up to date? I'm on the travel lift all day.
We do. You change prices yourself in seconds from your phone if you want to. Everything else is ours — keeping it online, keeping it safe, putting something new on it, your Google listing. If we need something from you, it's one text.
My customers are older. They're not booking online.
Some won't, and they'll keep calling you, and that's fine. But the transient coming through the canal is deciding on a phone with one bar of signal, and the guy shopping winterizing in October is comparing you to the yard down the lake at nine at night. Those two don't call. They just go somewhere else, and you never find out.
Can you get me to the top of Google?
No. Anybody who promises that is lying. What we do is build a site that loads fast, that Google can read, with your lake, your towns and your services spelled out, and put something new on it every month. If you're starting from a phone number or a dead address, that by itself makes a real difference.
I do boat repair, not slips. Does this still fit?
Yes, and it's a cleaner fit than you'd think. The service requests with photos and the numbered job tracking are the whole thing for a repair shop, plus winterizing pricing. You'd skip the slip map. Tell us what you actually run and we build it that way around.
What if I want out?
The site comes down, we hand you your content and your domain name, and that's that. Nothing gets held hostage.
