Privacy Policy

Reef Tank Copilot — an AInovations LLC app. Last updated: August 12, 2026.

Reef Tank Copilot ("the app," "we," "us") helps saltwater aquarium keepers log water parameters, track trends, and get AI-assisted health assessments of their corals and fish. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. We do not sell your personal information.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in (Apple, Google, or an organization email), we receive your email address and a user identifier to create and secure your account.
  • Tank data you enter. Water-test values, tanks, corals, notes, and related records you choose to log.
  • Photos you submit. Images of your tank, corals, or fish that you upload for AI diagnosis or coral growth timelines.
  • Approximate location (optional). Only if you use the "find reef stores near you" feature, and only while using the app, to show nearby stores. You can decline this and still use the rest of the app.
  • Diagnostic and usage data. Basic app events and crash information used to keep the app reliable, and a monthly count of AI diagnoses to enforce plan limits.
  • Purchase status. Whether you hold an active subscription (managed by the App Store / Google Play and our subscription provider). We do not receive your card or payment details.

How we use it

  • Provide core features: saving your logs, drawing trend charts, and computing your Tank Health Score.
  • Generate AI health assessments from the photos and notes you submit.
  • Enforce free/Pro plan limits and, on the free tier, show ads.
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and fix problems.

Service providers we share data with

We share only what each provider needs to deliver its part of the service:

  • Supabase — hosts your account and tank data (database, authentication, file storage).
  • Anthropic (Claude) — processes the photo and note you submit to produce an AI diagnosis. Images are sent for the purpose of generating your result.
  • Google AdMob — serves ads on the free tier. We request non-personalized ads where required and honor your consent choices.
  • RevenueCat and the App Store / Google Play — process subscriptions and report entitlement status.

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising beyond the ad functionality described above.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete account in the app. Deletion removes your account, tanks, logs, corals, and submitted photos from our systems. You may also email us to request deletion.

Your choices

  • Decline or revoke location access in your device settings.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store or Google Play account settings.
  • Adjust ad personalization consent when prompted.
  • Request access to or deletion of your data by contacting us.

Children

Reef Tank Copilot is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored with our hosting provider's access controls. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
