🐟 In-Store Compatibility Scanner

Scan a fish at the store. Know if it fits.

It's the most useful two seconds in fishkeeping. Before that impulse buy goes in the bag, scan it — and TankMate checks it against the tank you actually have at home.

What it checks

TankMate weighs the four things that decide whether a new fish thrives or causes chaos: adult size, water type and parameters, temperament, and your existing livestock. Not a generic care sheet — your tank.

Will these fish get along?

That's the question the scanner answers before you spend a dollar. It flags aggression, predation, and adult-size mismatches that a tiny juvenile in a store tank hides completely.

Real examples it catches

  • A cute juvenile lionfish that will eat your small tankmates once it grows.
  • A second male betta that can't share a tank with the one you have.
  • Caridina and Neocaridina shrimp that want different water and shouldn't mix.
  • A fish that needs far more gallons than your tank will ever hold.

Backed by a real compatibility engine

The warnings are deterministic and hobby-accurate — built on real fishkeeping rules, not vibes — so the answer you get at the store is one you can trust.
