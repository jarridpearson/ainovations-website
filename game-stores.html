Questions

The things you're actually thinking

Nobody's going to download a game store's app.
Some won't, and that's fine — the website serves them. But the people who come to your tournament nights and trade games in every month absolutely will, because it holds their points, their want list and their seat. You don't need 4,800 downloads. You need the 300 regulars who spend the most, and those are exactly the ones who'll take it.
I already have a rewards program.
Good — that's the hard part and you've done it. The problem with every counter-based program is that the customer forgets they're in it. This puts the balance on their phone and tells them when they're close to a reward. Same program, in a place they actually look.
Will this work with my POS?
If you're on Square, yes — we read your catalog and stock levels directly and you change nothing about how you ring people up. Lightspeed and Shopify also work. If you're on something else or you track stock on paper, tell us and we'll say honestly whether the stock-checking part can work for you before you sign anything.
How long until it's live?
The website is quick. The app is slower and it's mostly Apple and Google, not us — enrolling your business in their developer programs and getting through review usually takes four to six weeks. Text messaging needs carrier registration on top of that, which is another one to three weeks. Nothing goes live until you've approved it.
What do I actually have to do?
Enroll in the Apple and Google developer programs with us, in your business's name. Give us read access to your Square. Tell your regulars it exists — a sign at the counter and a card in the bag does most of it. After that you post events when you feel like it and send the occasional notification. Everything else is ours.
Do you take a cut of my sales?
No. Not of online orders, not of in-store sales, not of anything. The money runs through your Square account and lands in your bank. $499 a month is the whole amount you pay us.
What if I cancel?
The developer accounts, the app listing, your Square, your customer list and your domain were always yours and they stay yours. We hand over your content and stop billing. Nothing is held hostage.
Can you get me to the top of Google?
No, and anybody promising that is lying. What we do is build a fast site search engines can read, keep your Google listing accurate, and put something new on it regularly. That helps. It isn't a guarantee and we won't pretend it is.
