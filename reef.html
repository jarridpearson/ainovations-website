FAQ

Good to know.

Where can I get it?

Reef Tank Copilot is live now on Google Play — download it free on any Android phone or tablet. The iPhone and iPad version is coming soon to the App Store; the App Store button above will go live the moment it's available.

Do I need to test my water for it to work?

No — you can start with an AI photo diagnosis alone. But logging your parameters gives the AI more context and unlocks your Tank Health Score and trend charts.

Is my payment information safe?

Yes. All subscriptions are processed by the App Store and Google Play. The app and AInovations never receive your card details.

Can I manage more than one tank?

Yes. Multi-tank support lets you manage several tanks from one account and switch between them instantly.
