How to Play Kick a Donut

Everything a new kicker needs — from your first launch to the legendary hacker donut.

Kick a Donut is easy to pick up: train your kick, boot a donut for cash, and spend that cash to kick even farther. Here's how it all fits together.

The core loop: Train → Kick → Get Paid

  1. Train. Power up your kick so each donut flies farther.
  2. Kick. Launch the donut across the map — distance is everything.
  3. Get paid. Cash in on every launch, then buy sillier and stronger donuts to hit new records.

Unlock all 5 worlds

Spend your earnings to unlock five wild worlds, each with its own vibe:

  • 1. Sprinkle Plains — where every kicker starts.
  • 2. Chocolate Canyon — richer rewards, richer scenery.
  • 3. Candy Factory — sweet upgrades on the assembly line.
  • 4. Golden Galaxy — kick donuts into orbit.
  • 5. Hacker Grid — the final frontier for the best kickers.

Hatch 75+ epic pets

Open eggs to hatch from a collection of 75+ pets. Pets follow you around and boost your KICK and your CASH, so the more you hatch, the faster you grow. Rarer pets give bigger boosts.

Grab the rainbow trail

Show off with a glowing rainbow World Trail that streaks behind you as your donuts fly. It's all about style points.

Rebirth for permanent power

Once you've built up enough, rebirth to reset your run in exchange for permanent boosts. Each rebirth makes you stronger than before — keep going and you'll unlock the legendary "hacker donut."

Beginner tips

  • Reinvest early — upgrade your kick before splurging on cosmetics.
  • Hatch pets often; the kick + cash boosts stack up fast.
  • Don't fear your first rebirth — the permanent boost is worth it.
