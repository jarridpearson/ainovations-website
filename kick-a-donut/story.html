Our Story

A goofy little donut game with a whole lot of heart. 🍩

I'm a Dad, and this is a personal one. My two boys — 8 and 9 — told me they want to make video games someday, so instead of just saying "that's nice," we sat down and actually built one together.

Every idea in it is 100% theirs: you kick donuts across the map, they fly for cash, you buy sillier and stronger donuts, unlock new worlds, grab a glowing rainbow trail, and rebirth to start over even stronger — right down to the "hacker donut." I just wrote the code; the whole vision is them.

The honest part

Right now Roblox has the game locked to players 16 and up (plus a few trusted friends) — which stinks for a goofy little kids' donut game, because most of their own friends aren't even old enough to get in yet. The only way to lift that 16+ lock and open it up to everyone is to reach about 500 dedicated players in 30 days.

We're one small family with no following, so to two little kids that number feels impossible — and that's where I'm hoping people might help. If you've got two minutes, it would mean the world if you'd:

  • Play a round — even one kick helps.
  • Share it with anyone who likes goofy Roblox games.
  • Leave a comment — they read every single one and light up.

Oh, and every Robux earned is theirs, not mine — they deserve the rewards of their hard work. Their other dream? Each one wants a brand-new gas-powered dirt bike. LOL.

It's a small, silly donut game, but to an 8 and 9 year old it's their game, and every person who plays makes them grin ear to ear. Thank you for reading. 🍩
